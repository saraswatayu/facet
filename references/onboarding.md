# Facet

48 personas. Real behavioral psychology. One command.

You describe a product question. Facet generates a population of psychologically
detailed personas, simulates them through your decision, and tells you what happened.

## What It Does

You ask: "Should I charge $15 or $30/month?"

Facet generates 48 personas across 6 market segments. Each persona has a specific
income, decision style, existing subscriptions, and a reason they might say no.
Then it runs each one through your pricing decision with a four-step emotional arc:
gut reaction, building considerations, moment of doubt, resolution.

The output: which price won, why, and the 5 most interesting personas' reasoning.

## Study Types

| Type | Use when you're asking... |
|------|--------------------------|
| **pricing** | "How should I price this?" |
| **copy** | "Which messaging resonates?" |
| **features** | "Should we build this feature?" |
| **onboarding** | "Which signup flow works?" |
| **retention** | "Why are users churning?" |
| **custom** | Anything that doesn't fit above |

## Try It

```
/facet "Should I charge $15 or $30/month for my task management app?"
```

```
/facet "We're testing two onboarding flows: guided tour vs self-serve. Which keeps users?"
```

```
/facet "Is an AI time-estimation feature worth building for our project management tool?"
```

## What to Expect

- 2-3 quick questions about your users and market
- A study config for your approval before anything runs
- ~20 minutes of simulation time (48 personas, real behavioral psychology)
- A research panel introduction (5 standout personas)
- Top findings with honest confidence levels
- Follow-up options: explore any persona, run another study, export for stakeholders

## Honest Limitations

The personas are synthetic. The patterns are plausible; the people aren't. Facet
generates hypotheses, not evidence. Use it to sharpen your thinking and prepare
better questions for real user interviews. Not for final go/no-go decisions.
